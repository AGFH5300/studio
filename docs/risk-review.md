## Six-role review and resulting revisions

**Creative director:** an exploded stack alone would fail identity. Revised to an asymmetric joined folio, consistent offset spine and four functional moments. Limit scenes to one kit. Next visual gate checks silhouette without the logo; aesthetic success remains unproven until built.

**Senior frontend engineer:** too many state owners and renderer dependencies would be fragile. Revised to plain-TS domain engine, independent UI/scene state, one transform owner, stable manifests, safe share migration and server recomputation. Removed physics and mandatory WebGPU.

**UAE business owner:** a spectacular custom site can make the entry package feel unbelievable. Revised hero to expose the offering and price immediately, pricing to distinguish entry scope from flagship custom execution, and contact to allow direct inquiry without estimator completion. Included real-business content requirements rather than fabricated trust badges.

**Mobile visitor:** a cinematic scene plus fixed panels could consume the screen and interfere with typing. Revised to a compact collapsible illustration, full-width in-page questions, one unfolded leaf, safe-area-aware CTA, no forced orientation or gestures.

**Conversion specialist:** a lengthy builder could become a lead gate and unclear estimates could damage trust. Revised to ungated summary/copy/share, direct contact bypass, explicit unknown scope and immediate scope feedback. Success metric is qualified inquiry acceptance, not time playing.

**Performance engineer:** a small GLB does not mean a small site, and average fps hides stalls. Revised budgets to include code/poster/textures, specify decoded texture targets, inspect sustained frame times, stop idle rendering and preserve a complete Tier 0. Removed ambient video and default audio.
